Review the quiz questions used in this course. The correct answer is highlighted in green.

  1. Question 1

    What is the most common entry point for a phishing attack?

    • Physical access to the office
    • A fraudulent email asking the user to click a link
    • A USB drive left in the lobby
    • A phone call from someone pretending to be IT
  2. Question 2

    If you receive an unexpected email asking you to verify your password, what should you do first?

    • Click the link and enter your password to make sure your account is safe
    • Reply to the sender asking if it is legitimate
    • Report the email to IT or use your reporting tool, and do not click the link
    • Delete it and forget about it
  3. Question 3

    Which of the following is a sign that an email may be a phishing attempt?

    • The email is signed with a real-looking name
    • The sender's address matches a domain you trust
    • The message creates urgency or fear and pressures you to act quickly
    • The email is plain text with no attachments
  4. Question 4

    Why is multi-factor authentication (MFA) important against phishing?

    • It encrypts every email you send
    • Even if a password is stolen, an attacker still needs the second factor to log in
    • It blocks all phishing emails from arriving in your inbox
    • It is required by law in every country
  5. Question 5

    A colleague forwards you an email that looks suspicious. What is the safest next step?

    • Open the attachment to see if it is dangerous
    • Forward it to the whole team to warn them
    • Report it through the official reporting channel and avoid interacting with its contents
    • Delete your colleague's email without telling them
