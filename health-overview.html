The Psyber Stages

Cybersecurity awareness isn’t on the radar. There’s no training, no ownership, and no understanding that people are part of the organisation’s defence.

“We don’t train.”

Training happens occasionally, often just to check a box. There’s no consistency, and no long-term plan for awareness.

“We do it when we have time.”

A formal awareness program is in place. Roles are defined, and basic training is consistent across the organisation.

“We have a plan.”

Awareness is integrated into the business strategy. Metrics drive improvements, and security is seen as a business enabler.

“We manage security risk.”

Security is second nature. Everyone takes ownership, and the culture itself provides a powerful layer of defence.

“It’s just how we work.”
